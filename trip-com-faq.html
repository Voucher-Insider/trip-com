Do I need a Trip.com promo code?

The supplied guide says eligible new users can claim available coupons through an invitation link, without manually entering a separate code.

What discounts are listed for new users?

The guide lists 10% off Hotels & Homes, 5% off Trains, 10% off Attractions & Tours and 10% off Airport Transfers, subject to eligibility.

Is 60% off guaranteed?

No. Up to 60% off refers to selected rotating promotions, not every booking.

Where can I find claimed Trip.com coupons?

The guide says claimed discounts appear under Promo Codes in the Trip.com account.

Is this an official Trip.com website?

No. This is an independent informational site linking to TheSmart.Deals and Trip.com.
