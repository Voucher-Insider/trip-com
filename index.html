Quick guide

How to claim Trip.com new-user discounts

  1. Open the Trip.com new-user link.
  2. Sign in or create a new account, if eligible.
  3. Look for the available welcome coupons and choose Claim all where offered.
  4. Find claimed offers under your account’s Promo Codes section.
  5. Check eligible bookings and confirm the final discount at checkout.
